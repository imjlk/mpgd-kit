---
npm/@mpgd/capacitor-storekit: minor (Changed)
---

The iOS plugin now runs on the OpenIAP Apple core (`hyodotdev/openiap` 4.0.5, pinned exactly in `Package.swift`) instead of its own StoreKit 2 code. The Capacitor contract is unchanged: `CapacitorStoreKit`, its methods and `transactionUpdated` event, the payload fields, the `STOREKIT_*` codes, and the finish gate (verified, one-time, not revoked, matching app account token, only after the ledger grant; OpenIAP's auto-finish is never requested). `getTransactions` still lists verified unfinished one-time transactions plus current non-consumable entitlements (`unfinished: false`), never the full history. **Behavior to know:** `getTransactions` and `finishTransaction` now reject with `STOREKIT_TRANSACTION_UNVERIFIED` while StoreKit cannot verify a transaction on the device, and with `STOREKIT_EVIDENCE_INVALID` for a verified transaction whose JWS cannot be read, instead of skipping it (a failed read is never an empty or shortened list); the result of a `purchase` call is also emitted as `transactionUpdated` (the reconciler deduplicates); revocations, which OpenIAP does not deliver, still arrive through a small `Transaction.updates` shim. The package also declares the product `MpgdCapacitorStorekit`, the spelling `cap sync ios` links, so a synced shell no longer needs a patch. SwiftPM now resolves the OpenIAP monorepo on first sync. Rollback is pinning the previous plugin version and re-running `cap sync ios`; journals, evidence, and transaction ids are unchanged. Details: `docs/specs/commerce/openiap-migration.md`.
