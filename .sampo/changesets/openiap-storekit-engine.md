---
npm/@mpgd/capacitor-storekit: minor (Changed)
---

The iOS plugin now runs on the OpenIAP Apple core (`hyodotdev/openiap` 4.0.5, pinned exactly in `Package.swift`) instead of its own StoreKit 2 code. The Capacitor contract is unchanged: `CapacitorStoreKit`, its methods and `transactionUpdated` event, the payload fields, the `STOREKIT_*` codes, and the finish gate (verified, one-time, not revoked, matching app account token, only after the ledger grant; OpenIAP's auto-finish is never requested). `getTransactions` still lists verified unfinished one-time transactions plus current non-consumable entitlements (`unfinished: false`), never the full history. **Behavior to know:** `getTransactions` and the `finishTransaction` lookup read StoreKit's verified lists directly through a read-only shim (OpenIAP's queries fail as a whole on one unverifiable transaction), so an unverified transaction is skipped on its own as before and never blocks recovering or finishing the others; the lists stay unfiltered by account (the TypeScript SDK admits the session account). The result of a `purchase` call is also emitted as `transactionUpdated` (the reconciler deduplicates); revocations, which OpenIAP does not deliver, still arrive through a small `Transaction.updates` shim. The package also declares the product `MpgdCapacitorStorekit`, the spelling `cap sync ios` links, so a synced shell no longer needs a patch. SwiftPM now resolves the OpenIAP monorepo on first sync. Rollback is pinning the previous plugin version and re-running `cap sync ios`; journals, evidence, and transaction ids are unchanged. Details: `docs/specs/commerce/openiap-migration.md`.
