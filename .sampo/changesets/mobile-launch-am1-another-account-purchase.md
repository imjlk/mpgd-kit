---
npm/@mpgd/game-services: minor (Changed)
---

A verify whose store binding names another account is no longer a terminal rejection. When one player's session verifies another player's Google Play or App Store purchase, the reference verifiers answer `{ status: 'rejected', reason, anotherAccount: true }` (new `EvidenceVerificationDecision` field) once every other check passed, and `createGameServicesBackend` grants and records nothing and throws the new `GameServicesPurchaseBoundToAnotherAccountError` (`code` `PURCHASE_BOUND_TO_ANOTHER_ACCOUNT`, `reason`, `status` 409). The HTTP handler answers it as 409 `{ error: 'PURCHASE_BOUND_TO_ANOTHER_ACCOUNT', reason }` (new `GameServicesBackendErrorCode` member and `reason` field), the oRPC router as that error code with `data.reason` (409 through `createGameServicesRpcFetchHandler`), and analytics tracks `purchase_pending` with `boundToAnotherAccount`. A restorable purchase that restore sharing does not cover (policy `refuse`, a game-started checkout, a consumable) gets the same answer instead of `rejected`. Restore sharing, the holder limit, and restorable decisions are unchanged.

The commerce reconciler parks such a record instead of ending it: it stays `verifying` with the new `anotherAccount: { reason, reportedAt }` field, is never resent by start, resume, or event passes, is left out of `listRecoverable()`, summarizes as `rejected` for its player, and is sent again only by that player's Restore Purchases when the store reports the purchase again, with the evidence the store reports then. Its shared journal key no longer refuses another player of the install, who journals the purchase under its own player-scoped key (consumables included), so the purchase's own account recovers it on the same device. A `rejected` receipt with `GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH`, `GOOGLE_PLAY_PROFILE_MISMATCH`, or `APP_STORE_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_MISMATCH` (an older backend, or a game server that maps every refusal to `rejected`) is parked the same way, and a terminal record from an older client with one of those reasons also leaves the key to the owner. New exports: `purchaseBoundToAnotherAccountCode`, `purchaseBoundToAnotherAccountStatus`, `storeAccountMismatchReasons`, `isStoreAccountMismatchReason`, `readPurchaseBoundToAnotherAccount`, `createPurchaseBoundToAnotherAccountBody`, `isParkedForAnotherAccount`.

A purchase bound to no account is no account's and stays `rejected`, now with its own reason so the mismatch reasons mean only "another account": `GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_ID_MISSING` and `GOOGLE_PLAY_PROFILE_ID_MISSING` (was `GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` / `GOOGLE_PLAY_PROFILE_MISMATCH`), `GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_BINDING_UNEXPECTED` for a bound purchase on a backend that binds nobody (was `GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH`), and `APP_STORE_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_MISSING` (was `APP_STORE_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_MISMATCH`). An App Store transaction with another token whose other checks fail (revoked, wrong product type) now reports that check's reason instead of the token mismatch.

Released clients (0.27 to 0.31) treat the 409 as a transport failure: the record stays `verifying` and is resent on later passes, instead of ending as `rejected`. A game server that wraps the verifiers should pass the answer through: catch `GameServicesPurchaseBoundToAnotherAccountError` from `backend.purchases.verifyPurchase` (or check `decision.anotherAccount === true` when it calls a verifier itself) and answer 409 with `createPurchaseBoundToAnotherAccountBody(reason)` instead of a `rejected` receipt; see "Another account's purchase" in `docs/GAME_SERVICES_BACKEND.md`.
