npm/@mpgd/game-services: patch (Fixed)
---

In `@mpgd/game-services/notifications`, a scheduler replay that ends without a provider call and puts an attempted row back as it was (a host or store read failed before the call, or a bulk batch left the row out) now keeps the time the gate counts the earlier attempt from. A row stored without its attempt time (`attemptedAt`; the service writes one at every claim, so this is a row a store kept without it) is counted from its send's `changedAt`, and writing the row back moved that to the replay's claim: the attempt then counted toward `frequency.maxPerDay` and the provider limits for longer than a day after it, and could hold back a send that was due. The attempt time is now written out with the row, as a replay that cannot keep its attempt already did. Every way the replay ends its claim on a row also goes through one helper now, which keeps the earlier attempt whenever the change stops the gate counting it (the row is dropped or waits deferred), with the failure handling as before: before the provider call, a record that cannot be written fails the run and puts the row back; after it, the failure is reported and the row settles. The service passes the new `notice-given-keeps-attempt` conformance vector.
