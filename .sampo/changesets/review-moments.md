npm/@mpgd/platform: minor (Added)
---

`@mpgd/platform/reviews-runtime` now decides when to ask for a review, not only how often. `createReviewSession({ gateway, gameId })` wraps `createReviewController` for one game runtime: it reads the gateway's review provider, keeps state under `mpgd:reviews:v1:<gameId>:<target>:<providerId>`, applies `defaultReviewPolicy` (one visit, two completed plays, fourteen days between requests) unless the game passes its own, reports observations through `onEvent` by the names in `reviewAnalyticsEvents` (`review_request`, `review_request_resolved`, `review_request_failed`, `review_storage_unavailable`), and returns `undefined` where the gateway has no native prompt. It checks the game ID and the policy on every target, so a mistake fails on the first web run too. `createReviewMoments(session)` takes `record(momentId)` for every finished play, whatever its outcome, or a reward the backend already granted, and `settled(isSafe)` when the player reaches a settled screen: a new moment opens one opportunity, a covered screen keeps it for the next settled screen, and the safety check runs again after every wait. `reviewMomentId(prefix)` makes an ID for a moment without its own. Games that do not call them are unchanged; a game with its own layer over `createReviewController` and the scope `<gameId>:<target>` can switch and keep its stored visits, plays and cooldown. `docs/REVIEWS.md` shows the wiring and the anti-patterns.
