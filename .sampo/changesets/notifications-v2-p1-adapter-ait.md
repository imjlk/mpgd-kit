Serve notifications v2 on Apps in Toss. The host bridge answers the `notifications.v2.*` methods from a provider over `Notification.requestAgreement` (Toss 5.255.0 or later), configured with the new `notificationTopics` option (`templateSetCode` and `agreement` per topic; v1 `notificationTemplateCodes` become agreement-required topics), and the gateway exposes `notificationsV2` whenever the host offers the notifications event channel, which the host does while a topic is mapped; the game side loads the bridge client and its validators lazily, on first use. Agreement answers map to `granted`, `already-granted`, and `denied`, a sheet without an answer within 120 seconds to `dismissed`, `TERMS_DISAGREED_MEMBER` to `action-required` (`host-settings`), and each decision carries `apps-in-toss.notification-agreement.v1` evidence for the backend's consent record. A launch whose referrer is `push` or `inbox` is routed exactly once, by whichever comes first: a cold `opened` event with its landing route, after which `presentation.getLaunchIntent()` answers `home`, or the launch intent itself; this applies while `notificationTopics` maps a topic (games that map only v1 template codes keep their launch routes). The v1 `notifications` methods are now served from the same provider; a topic agreed to during the bridge's lifetime reads `subscribed` from a v1-only session cache, as before, and v2 reads stay `unknown` (`unsupported`, and `requestSubscription` `unavailable`, on Toss older than 5.255.0). The `requestNotificationAgreement` host dependency is now typed as and defaults to `Notification.requestAgreement`, a new `readInitialUrl` dependency reads `Environment.initialURL` (the page URL serves where the host gives none), and `notificationAgreementTimeoutMs` may shorten the 120-second settle. Without any mapped topic the gateway offers no `notificationsV2`, and the host's v2 methods answer `configuration-required` capabilities. `@mpgd/adapter-ait/local-mock` now exports `Notification` and `Environment` in place of `requestNotificationAgreement`. In the kit's Apps in Toss staging build the game's eagerly loaded chunks grow by about 2 kB gzip, the lazily loaded notifications chunk is about 18 kB gzip, and the wrapper bundle, which serves the host side, grows by about 19 kB gzip.
