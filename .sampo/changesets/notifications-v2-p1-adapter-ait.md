Serve notifications v2 on Apps in Toss. The host bridge answers the `notifications.v2.*` methods from a provider over `Notification.requestAgreement` (Toss 5.255.0 or later), configured with the new `notificationTopics` option (`templateSetCode` and `agreement` per topic; v1 `notificationTemplateCodes` become agreement-required topics), and the gateway exposes `notificationsV2` whenever the host offers the notifications event channel. Agreement answers map to `granted`, `already-granted`, and `denied`, a sheet without an answer within 120 seconds to `dismissed`, `TERMS_DISAGREED_MEMBER` to `action-required` (`host-settings`), and each decision carries `apps-in-toss.notification-agreement.v1` evidence for the backend's consent record. A launch whose referrer is `push` or `inbox` is routed exactly once, by whichever comes first: a cold `opened` event with its landing route, after which `presentation.getLaunchIntent()` answers `home`, or the launch intent itself; this applies while `notificationTopics` maps a topic (games that map only v1 template codes keep their launch routes). The v1 `notifications` methods are now served from the same provider; a topic agreed to during the bridge's lifetime reads `subscribed` from a v1-only session cache, as before, and v2 reads stay `unknown` (`unsupported`, and `requestSubscription` `unavailable`, on Toss older than 5.255.0). The `requestNotificationAgreement` host dependency is now typed as and defaults to `Notification.requestAgreement`, a new `readInitialUrl` dependency reads `Environment.initialURL` (the page URL serves where the host gives none), and `notificationAgreementTimeoutMs` may shorten the 120-second settle. Without any mapped topic the gateway still offers `notificationsV2`, whose capabilities read `configuration-required`. `@mpgd/adapter-ait/local-mock` now exports `Notification` and `Environment` in place of `requestNotificationAgreement`. The game and wrapper bundles now carry the notifications v2 bridge validators (about 16 kB gzip in the game bundle and 19 kB in the wrapper for the kit's Apps in Toss staging build).
