Restore sharing: a verified restore of a non-consumable that the store binds to another player (a reinstall on Android, a new iOS device, a second device on the same store account, a logout) can grant the restoring player its own entitlement while earlier holders keep theirs.

- `createGameServicesBackend` takes `restoreSharing` (`PurchaseRestoreSharingPolicy`): `{ mode: 'share', maxHolders? }` (default 5 players per purchase, 1 to 100) or `{ mode: 'refuse' }`, the default, which keeps answering the binding mismatch. `sharePurchaseRestores`, `refusePurchaseRestoreSharing`, `resolvePurchaseRestoreSharingPolicy`, and `isRetainedPurchasePlayerId` are exported.
- `VerifyPurchaseRequest.intent?: 'purchase' | 'restore'` (validated); the commerce reconciler sends `restore` for purchases it discovered from the store. `VerifyPurchaseResponse.restoredAcrossPlayers?: true` marks a grant restored from a purchase another player made or holds.
- `EvidenceVerificationDecision` gains `{ status: 'restorable', reason, ... }`. The Google Play boundary answers it for a restore of a non-consumable whose only failed check is the account binding (`GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` / `GOOGLE_PLAY_PROFILE_MISMATCH`, including a purchase with no ids); the App Store verifier for `APP_STORE_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_MISMATCH` when the client's StoreKit-verified JWS is present. Every other request is answered as before. Ad reward claims and `createAppStoreRecoveryBackend` treat it as a refusal; code that switches on `status` exhaustively must handle it.
- Ledger: shares are ordinary grants (no store change, no migration): evidence id `createPurchaseHolderEvidenceVerificationId(purchaseId, slot)` at the next free slot, key `createPurchaseHolderIdempotencyKey(purchaseId)` per player, so the holder limit holds when restores race. `listPurchaseHolders`, `readPurchaseVerificationId`, and `isRestoredAcrossPlayers` are exported. `revokeEntitlementGrant` with a store reason (`refund`, `chargeback`, `voided`) on a purchase grant now also revokes every other holder of the purchase; an `admin` revocation stays on its grant.
- Answers: `verified` with `restoredAcrossPlayers: true`; `pending` `PURCHASE_HOLDER_LIMIT_REACHED` at the limit (nobody is evicted) and `PURCHASE_HOLDER_CONFLICT` after repeated lost races; `rejected` `LEDGER_GRANT_REVERSED` for a refunded purchase. Consumables and subscriptions are never shared.
