Android advertising pushes rendered by the shell (AR1). A device registration's FCM address can say `rendersAdvertising: true`: the app renders advertising pushes itself, with the opt-out action. `NotificationDeployment.shellRendersAdvertising` says the game's shell does so on the targets in `notificationShellRenderedAdvertisingTargets` (`android`, whose FCM notification messages cannot carry actions). The policy gate takes `endpointShowsActions` and fails closed on it: under the Korean rules an advertising send to a resolved device endpoint needs `true`, and `false` or an absent value is `suppressed: purpose-unsupported`, as a registry entry without `actions` is. A backend that calls `evaluateNotificationDelivery` itself passes `true` for an endpoint whose pushes show the opt-out action. The conformance runner's fake backend does so for its fixture device; no vector outcome changed. The JSON Schemas for the endpoint registration and the deployment carry both fields.
