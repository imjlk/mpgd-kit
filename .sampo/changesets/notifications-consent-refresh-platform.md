A consent refresh policy for advertising and night consent (RFC "Consent refresh", open question 6). `NotificationConsentRefreshRule` (`intervalMonths`, `noticeLeadDays`, optional `pushLeadDays`, `onOverdue: 'pause' | 'continue'`) per kind makes up a `NotificationConsentRefreshPolicy`, which a deployment outside the Korean rules may set as `consentRefresh`; under `kr` the rules fix it to `notificationKoreanConsentRefresh` (every 24 months, listed 60 and pushed 7 days before `dueBy`, pausing once overdue), and `resolveNotificationConsentRefresh` and `assertNotificationDeployment` refuse a `consentRefresh` there. `NotificationNoticePolicy`, `notificationKoreanNoticePolicy`, and `resolveNotificationNoticePolicy` say which notices a deployment owes; `listNotificationNotices` takes a policy (the Korean one by default, so existing calls list exactly what they did), `notificationNoticePushFrom` gives a notice's first push day, `notificationConsentRefreshDueBy` dates a refresh of any interval, `notificationOverdueConsentRefreshes` names the overdue kinds, and `notificationConsentRefreshIssues` checks a policy.

**Behaviour change under `kr`.** The policy gate takes `noticesGiven` and, where the refresh pauses (the Korean rules do), suppresses an advertising send as the new `reconfirmation-overdue` once the player's advertising re-confirmation is past its `dueBy` unrecorded, until the notice is recorded or the player consents anew; an overdue night re-confirmation counts as no night consent, which holds night-time advertising only. Absent, `noticesGiven` reads as none: the gate fails closed. Notice names, keys, variants, and dates are unchanged. The conformance vector and deployment schemas list the new reason and field, and the vector `advertising-reconfirmation-overdue` is new; a consumer that validates delivery outcomes against a closed reason list should accept the new reason.

**Migration for backends that call `evaluateNotificationDelivery` directly under `kr`.** After this upgrade such a backend pauses advertising for every player whose advertising (or night) consent is older than 24 months and has not been re-consented, unless it passes the notices it recorded. The fix is one line: pass the player's recorded notices (`notices.recordShown` records and delivered notice pushes, the `NotificationNoticeIdentity` list your store keeps) as `noticesGiven: await store.listNoticesGiven(player)` in the gate input. The fail-closed default is deliberate. `@mpgd/game-services`' notification service already passes them.

`notices.copy` checks take the variants the deployment requires (`notificationNoticeCopyIssues`'s new `required` argument, `notificationRequiredNoticeVariants`): under `kr`, and without a refresh of its own, every variant but a refusal as before; with a `consentRefresh` outside `kr`, only the `reconfirmation.*` texts it lists.

`listNotificationNotices` now lists every re-confirmation of a consent that is past its `dueBy` and not given, not only the first, so a consent past two due dates lists both and giving the listed ones ends a pause at once; dates and names are unchanged, and the normal case (at most one overdue) lists exactly what it did. `notificationUpcomingReconfirmations` gives the next re-confirmation per kind for a scheduler. `NotificationNoticeLocaleCopy`'s keys are optional, since which variants a catalog must give depends on its deployment (`notificationRequiredNoticeVariants`, which `notificationNoticeCopyIssues` checks); a reader looks a variant up through `notificationNoticeVariantCopy`. `notificationConsentRefreshDueBy` refuses an interval over 120 months, `notificationConsentRefreshIssues` reports a notice lead of `28 × intervalMonths − 1` days or more, and the notice dates no longer read years 0 to 99 as 1900 to 1999.
