Add bulk notification sends (notifications v2 RFC open question 5). `NotificationService.deliverBulk(input, options)` in `@mpgd/game-services/notifications` sends one topic to many players through one provider: each player is a single send with its own key (`<idempotencyKey>:<encodeURIComponent(player)>`), ledger row, policy-gate check, and answer, and the provider gets shared calls. A delivery provider declares a bulk path with `bulk` (`NotificationRemoteBulkCapability`: `maxRecipients`, `minRecipients`, `perRecipientOutcomes`, `holdWindow`) and `sendBatch`; one without it keeps sending one recipient per call. A retry of the same call answers players already sent from the ledger, and players over the call's time or recipient budget, or over the provider's `rate`, answer `not-attempted` and leave nothing behind. A bulk answer that proves only that some recipients were reached is `ambiguous` with `unattributed`: the row stays in flight and is never replayed, so no player gets the message twice. The Apps in Toss functional-message provider sends 50 to 2,500 recipients through `send-bulk-message` (one by one below 50 and from 21:00 to 08:00 Seoul time, when the host would hold a bulk message). In `@mpgd/adapter-devvit/server`, the Devvit provider batches up to 1,000 recipients per `enqueue` and maps Devvit's per-user errors and counts per recipient, and `runDevvitNotificationBulkPage` sends one `listOptedInUsers` page through `deliverBulk` with the kit's gate still deciding each user.
