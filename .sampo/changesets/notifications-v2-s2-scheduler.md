Add server reminders and the notifications scheduler to `@mpgd/game-services/notifications`. `setReminder` and `cancelReminder` are the backend half of the runtime's `reminders.set` and `reminders.cancel`: gate steps 3 to 5 run when a reminder is set, a refusal stores nothing, and one reminder per player and key is kept, so a replace or a cancel also stops a send of the previous one that still waits. `runScheduler` fires due reminders as `reminder:` sends through the policy gate, pushes the consent notices nobody recorded by seven days before they are due through the catalog's notice topic under the Korean rules (recorded as given once delivered; a refused push is tried again after an hour, doubling to a day), and then dispatches due rows at no more than each provider's registry `rate`, through the dispatch queue when one is bound. Each consent write compacts the player's history once records are a day old, keeping the latest of each kind and scope and the advertising and night records a notice still needs. The first ambiguous replay now waits the claim lease instead of twice it. The service passes every conformance vector except the per-send one.

A custom `NotificationStore` implements six more steps, pinned by `runNotificationStoreConformance`: `getScheduleEntry`, `createScheduleEntry`, `updateScheduleEntry`, and `listDueScheduleEntries` (scheduler entries with a version compare-and-set and a due index), `countHistory` (a provider's deliveries since an instant, for pacing), and `pruneConsent` (deletes records by instant with the notices given about them). `createInMemoryNotificationStore` now refuses a time that does not parse, as the game-services Worker's D1 store does.
