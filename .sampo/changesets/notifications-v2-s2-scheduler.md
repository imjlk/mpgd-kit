Add server reminders and the notifications scheduler to `@mpgd/game-services/notifications`. `setReminder` and `cancelReminder` are the backend half of the runtime's `reminders.set` and `reminders.cancel`: gate steps 3 to 5 run when a reminder is set, a refusal stores nothing, and one reminder per player and key is kept, so a replace or a cancel also stops a send of the previous one that still waits. `runScheduler` fires due reminders as `reminder:` sends through the policy gate, pushes the consent notices nobody recorded by seven days before they are due through the catalog's notice topic under the Korean rules (recorded as given once delivered; a refused push is tried again after an hour, doubling to a day), and then dispatches due rows at no more than each provider's registry `rate`, through the dispatch queue when one is bound. Each consent write compacts the player's history once records are a day old, keeping the latest of each kind and scope and the advertising and night records a notice still needs. The first ambiguous replay now waits the claim lease instead of twice it. With a dispatch queue bound, `dispatchDue` takes due rows for the queue (their result is `in-flight`) instead of sending them inline, and a queue job runs any row no holder claims; the `onError` context gains `fromProvider` for a provider's own failure, whose message a host should not log; `InMemoryNotificationStoreState` gains `schedule`. Consent records are no longer kept whole: compaction deletes the ones no rule reads. The service passes every conformance vector except the per-send one.

A player keeps at most `maxPendingNotificationReminders` (64) pending server reminders (a new key past them answers `capacity-exceeded`) with a `fireAt` at most `notificationReminderHorizonDays` (90) ahead; a set answers `unavailable` when no provider the player can be reached on maps the topic, and checks a reminder's link as the gate does. A scheduler run goes on in batches until nothing is due or its time budget (`budgetMs`, 20 seconds by default) is used.

A custom `NotificationStore` implements seven more steps, pinned by `runNotificationStoreConformance`: `getScheduleEntry`, `createScheduleEntry`, `updateScheduleEntry`, and `listDueScheduleEntries` (scheduler entries with a version compare-and-set and a due index), `countPendingReminders` (a player's pending server reminders, for the cap), `countHistory` (a provider's deliveries since an instant, for pacing), and `pruneConsent` (deletes records by instant with the notices given about them). `createInMemoryNotificationStore` now refuses a time that does not parse, as the game-services Worker's D1 store does.
