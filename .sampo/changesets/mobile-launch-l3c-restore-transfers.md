npm/@mpgd/game-services: minor (Added)
---

Restore transfers: a verified restore of a non-consumable that the store binds to an earlier player (a reinstall on Android, a new iOS device, a logout) can move the grant to the restoring player.

- `createGameServicesBackend` takes `restoreTransfers` (`PurchaseRestoreTransferPolicy`): `{ mode: 'move', maxTransfersPerWindow?, windowMs? }` (defaults 3 moves per purchase in 30 days) or `{ mode: 'refuse' }`, the default, which keeps answering the binding mismatch. `movePurchaseRestoreTransfers`, `refusePurchaseRestoreTransfers`, and `resolvePurchaseRestoreTransferPolicy` are exported.
- `VerifyPurchaseRequest.intent?: 'purchase' | 'restore'` (validated); the commerce reconciler sends `restore` for purchases it discovered from the store. `VerifyPurchaseResponse.transferred?: true` marks a grant moved to this player.
- `EvidenceVerificationDecision` gains `{ status: 'restorable', reason, ... }`. The Google Play boundary answers it for a restore of a non-consumable whose only failed check is the account binding (`GOOGLE_PLAY_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH` / `GOOGLE_PLAY_PROFILE_MISMATCH`, including a purchase with no ids); the App Store verifier for `APP_STORE_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_MISMATCH` when the client's StoreKit-verified JWS is present. Every other request is answered as before. Ad reward claims and `createAppStoreRecoveryBackend` treat it as a refusal.
- Ledger: `EntitlementRevocationReason` gains `transfer`, written only by the new optional `GameServicesStore.recordEntitlementTransfer` (`EntitlementLedgerTransfer`, `EntitlementTransferResult`, `assertEntitlementLedgerTransfer`, `EntitlementTransferConflictError`), which appends the transfer entry and the receiving grant together; the in-memory store implements it, and its `recordEntitlementRevocation` refuses reason `transfer`. `findPurchaseGrantByEvidenceVerificationId` now answers the grant that holds the purchase after moves (`{ followTransfers: false }` for the first grant), and `revokeEntitlementGrant` on a moved grant reverses the current holder, so Play voided purchases and App Store refunds reach it. New helpers: `resolvePurchaseGrantHolder`, `readPurchaseVerificationId`, `isPurchaseTransferGrant`, `createPurchaseTransferEvidenceVerificationId`.
- Answers: `verified` with `transferred: true`; `pending` `PURCHASE_TRANSFER_RATE_LIMITED` past the limit and `PURCHASE_TRANSFER_CONFLICT` for a lost race; `rejected` `LEDGER_GRANT_REVERSED` for a refunded purchase. Consumables and subscriptions never move.
- Integration notes: `EvidenceVerificationDecision` has a new variant, so code that switches on `status` exhaustively must handle `restorable`; `revokeEntitlementGrant` throws for reason `transfer` and answers `{ status: 'grant_transferred', holderLedgerEntryId }` for an `admin` revocation of a moved grant; a custom `GameServicesStore` needs `recordEntitlementTransfer` for `move` to take effect (without it restores keep being refused).
