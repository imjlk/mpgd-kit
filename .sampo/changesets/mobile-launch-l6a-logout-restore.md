Restore Purchases works after a logout on the same install. The commerce reconciler's journal key of a purchase discovered from the store (`createCommercePurchaseOperationKey(target, derivedKey)`) is the same for every player of the install, so the next guest's restore of a non-consumable the earlier guest had journaled failed as "bound to another player" and sent nothing. On the player's Restore Purchases, a discovered durable whose key another player's record holds is now journaled under the new player-scoped key (`createCommercePurchaseOperationKey(target, derivedKey, playerId)`) and sent as this player's restore under the same derived idempotency key; the backend's restore sharing decides it. Start, resume, and event passes, and consumables, subscriptions, products of unknown kind, and game-started keys held by another player, stay refused: one player's purchase is never sent for another, and a purchase a normal read lists for the earlier player (an unacknowledged Google Play purchase) is never shared without the player asking. Existing journals need no migration: records keep their keys and each player still finds its own. `reconciler.read()` also answers this player's record under the player-scoped key and still refuses any other key another player's record holds. A custom `MonetizationOperationStore` must treat `record.key` as opaque; see `docs/MONETIZATION_RECOVERY.md`.
