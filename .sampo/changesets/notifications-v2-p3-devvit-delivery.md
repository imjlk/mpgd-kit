npm/@mpgd/adapter-devvit: minor (Added)
---

`@mpgd/adapter-devvit/server` adds notifications v2 delivery inside the Devvit server. `createDevvitNotificationDeliveryProvider` sends one recipient per `notifications.enqueue` call with the gate's filled title and body and the send's post or comment `link`, and reads `isOptedIn` at send time. It refuses before sending a title over 60 or a body over 100 Unicode code points of the filled text, a link that is not a `t3_` or `t1_` id, and a filled text containing `{{`, which Devvit's Mustache would read as a tag. `failureCount` and `errors` map per recipient: a message that reads as a rate limit is `rate-limited`, one that names the recipient and reads as the user's refusal is `rejected` with reason `recipient`, any other is `rejected` with reason `configuration`, and a call that throws, or an answer that counts nothing, is `ambiguous`. `classifyEnqueueError` overrides the defaults, and `onEnqueueError` sees every failure with Devvit's message; the service forwards the ambiguous ones and the configuration rejections to `onError`. `createDevvitNotificationProviderEntry` is the registry entry, with 2 per user per day as a provider limit and 25,000 per app per day as the `rate`. `createDevvitNotificationService` runs the `@mpgd/game-services/notifications` service with the Redis store and the provider for the `reddit` target, and `devvitNotificationSession(userId)` gives a logged-in user's session. The main entry exports the Devvit profile values (`createDevvitNotificationCapabilities`, the length limits and `devvitNotificationTextLength`).
