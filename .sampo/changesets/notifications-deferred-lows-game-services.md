npm/@mpgd/game-services: minor (Added)
---

`createGameServicesNotificationsClient().getProviders()` leaves out a provider entry it cannot read (a malformed one, or one a Worker shaped differently) and returns the others, instead of failing the whole registry with `NOTIFICATIONS_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED`. What was left out is listed in the new optional `unreadable` field of `GameServicesNotificationProviders` (`GameServicesNotificationUnreadableProvider[]`: the entry's `provider` id when it has a readable one, and the `issue`, at most 512 characters), in registry order and absent when nothing was left out. A registry that is not `{ protocolVersion, providers, targets }` still fails as a whole. A caller that loads a runtime's registry entry should report a target whose provider is listed in `unreadable` (or any unreadable entry without an id) rather than read it as a target the Worker does not serve. Error text built from a thrown value whose getter, `toString`, or `Proxy` trap throws now reads `a failure that cannot be read` instead of throwing.
