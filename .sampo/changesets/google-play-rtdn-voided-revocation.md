Google Play Real-time developer notifications (RTDN) revoke a refunded or voided one-time purchase as soon as Google pushes it, instead of waiting for the next Voided Purchases API sweep. New module `@mpgd/game-services/google-play-rtdn` (also exported from the package root):

- `verifyGooglePubSubPushToken` authenticates a Cloud Pub/Sub push with authentication: the `Authorization: Bearer` OIDC token's RS256 signature against Google's published keys, the issuer (`https://accounts.google.com` or `accounts.google.com`), the subscription's audience, its service account (`email`, `email_verified`), and `exp`/`iat` with a minute of clock skew. An empty audience or service account throws (fail closed). Keys come through the `GoogleIdTokenSigningKeys` port; `createGoogleIdTokenSigningKeys` reads `https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/certs`, caches it for the response's `max-age` (one minute to a day), refreshes early for an unknown key id at most once a minute, keeps verifying with a key it fetched before while the endpoint is down, and throws `GoogleIdTokenKeysUnavailableError` otherwise.
- `handleGooglePlayDeveloperNotification` decodes the wrapped Pub/Sub envelope (`decodeGooglePlayDeveloperNotificationPush`) and its base64 `DeveloperNotification`, checks `packageName`, and revokes a `voidedPurchaseNotification` of a one-time product with a full refund through `revokeGooglePlayVoidedPurchase`, the sweep's path and idempotency key: a redelivery and a later sweep of the same token answer `alreadyProcessed`. A quantity-based partial refund, a voided subscription, test, subscription, and one-time product notifications, and another package are `ignored`; a malformed message is `rejected`. A void whose grant this ledger does not hold yet is `retry` for `unmatchedVoidRetryMs` after the event (`defaultUnmatchedVoidRetryMs`, 1 hour; at most `maxUnmatchedVoidRetryMs`, 6 hours), then `ignored`.
- `googlePlayDeveloperNotificationHttpStatus` answers `503` for `retry` (Pub/Sub redelivers) and `200` for every other outcome, so a message a redelivery cannot change is acknowledged.

An RTDN carries no `voidedReason`: a void it revokes first is recorded as `voided` at the notification's event time (never later than the handler's clock), and a later sweep answers that entry rather than relabelling a chargeback. The Voided Purchases API sweep stays required next to RTDN: Pub/Sub delivery can be lost. The reference Worker mounts the route at `POST /google-play/rtdn` with `GAME_SERVICES_GOOGLE_PLAY_RTDN_AUDIENCE` and `GAME_SERVICES_GOOGLE_PLAY_RTDN_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` (see `docs/CLOUDFLARE_WORKER_DEPLOY.md`).
