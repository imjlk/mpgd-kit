Google Play Real-time developer notifications (RTDN) revoke a refunded or voided one-time purchase as soon as Google pushes it, instead of waiting for the next Voided Purchases API sweep. New module `@mpgd/game-services/google-play-rtdn` (also exported from the package root):

- `verifyGooglePubSubPushToken` authenticates a Cloud Pub/Sub push with authentication: the `Authorization: Bearer` OIDC token's RS256 signature against Google's published keys, the issuer (`https://accounts.google.com` or `accounts.google.com`), the subscription's audience, its service account (`email`, `email_verified`), and `exp`/`iat` with a minute of clock skew. An empty audience or service account throws (fail closed). Keys come through the `GoogleIdTokenSigningKeys` port; `createGoogleIdTokenSigningKeys` reads `https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/certs`, caches it for the response's `max-age` (one minute to a day), refreshes early for an unknown key id at most once a minute, keeps verifying with a key it fetched before while the endpoint is down, and throws `GoogleIdTokenKeysUnavailableError` otherwise.
- `handleGooglePlayDeveloperNotification` decodes the push, wrapped (the Pub/Sub envelope with its base64 `DeveloperNotification`) or unwrapped (payload unwrapping: the notification is the body; its message id is passed from the `x-goog-pubsub-message-id` header), and checks `packageName`. A `voidedPurchaseNotification` of a one-time product with a full refund is a trigger: the token is looked up with the required `voidedPurchases` client (the sweep's `GooglePlayVoidedPurchasesClient`; one query over a window from `googlePlayRtdnLookupLeadMs`, 15 minutes, before the event time to the earlier of now and `maxUnmatchedVoidRetryMs`, 6 hours, after it, at most `googlePlayRtdnLookupMaxPages`, 2) and the listed entry is revoked through `revokeGooglePlayVoidedPurchase`, so the ledger records the sweep's reason, source, void time, and key, a forged push cannot revoke anything Google did not void, and a redelivery or a later sweep answers `alreadyProcessed`. A void not listed yet, or whose grant this ledger does not hold yet, is `retry` for `unmatchedVoidRetryMs` after the event (`defaultUnmatchedVoidRetryMs`, 1 hour; at most `maxUnmatchedVoidRetryMs`, 6 hours), then `ignored`; a lookup failure is `retry`. A quantity-based partial refund, a voided subscription, test, subscription, and one-time product notifications, and another package are `ignored` without a lookup; a present `productType` or `refundType` that does not parse is `ignored` as unknown (an absent `refundType` stays a full refund); a malformed message is `rejected`.
- `googlePlayDeveloperNotificationHttpStatus` answers `503` for `retry` (Pub/Sub redelivers) and `200` for every other outcome, so a message a redelivery cannot change is acknowledged.

The Voided Purchases API sweep stays required next to RTDN: Pub/Sub delivery can be lost. Google allows 6,000 Voided Purchases API queries a day and 30 in any 30 seconds per package, shared with the sweep. `createGooglePlayPublisherClient` now bounds its access-token wait by the request's signal, so a token source that ignores the signal (a service binding) cannot hold a request past its deadline: it fails with an `AbortError`. `importAdMobSsvPublicKey` now refuses a key that is not base64 with an `Error` (`base64Spki must be base64.`) instead of the platform's `atob` exception; ASCII whitespace is still accepted. The reference Worker mounts the route at `POST /google-play/rtdn` with `GAME_SERVICES_GOOGLE_PLAY_RTDN_AUDIENCE` and `GAME_SERVICES_GOOGLE_PLAY_RTDN_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` (see `docs/CLOUDFLARE_WORKER_DEPLOY.md`).
