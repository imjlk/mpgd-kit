npm/@mpgd/cli: minor (Added)
---

Google Play releases beyond the internal track. `mpgd deploy submit` and `mpgd deploy run` take `--track` (`internal` by default, `alpha`, `beta`, `production`, a closed-testing track name, or a form-factor track), `--status draft|completed|inProgress`, `--user-fraction` for a staged rollout, `--release-notes` (Play Console `<en-US>…</en-US>` blocks or JSON, 500 characters per language) and `--confirm-production`, which production releases and staged rollouts require; `deploy submit` also takes `--mapping-file` (uploaded with `edits.deobfuscationfiles.upload`) and `--dry-run`. A profile's `preapproved-internal-test` approval covers only the internal track. The new release supersedes an in-progress or halted rollout (a draft supersedes an older draft); every other release on the track is sent back unchanged, and no other track is touched. When Play answers "Only releases with status draft may be created on draft app.", the release is re-staged and committed as a draft, with `releaseStatus: "draft"` and `draftAppFallback: true` in the result, for a first rollout from Play Console. The new `mpgd deploy rollout --action update|halt|resume|complete` changes a release already on a track without a bundle. Android submission checkpoints record a `playReleaseSha256` of the release options so a resumed edit is never committed with other options; without release flags the internal submission, its track body and its checkpoint are unchanged. New exports: `createPlayReleaseRequest`, `parsePlayReleaseNotes`, `planVerifiedAndroidBundleSubmission`, `planRecordedAndroidSubmission`, `changePlayRollout`, `PlayRolloutUncertainError` and their types; `PlayInternalSubmissionResult` gains `releaseStatus` and `draftAppFallback`, and its `track` is a string.
