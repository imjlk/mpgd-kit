Information We Process

  • Player identifiers. A random guest player ID issued by our game server, a random ID for this installation of the game, and, when you sign in, the account ID of the sign-in provider. Store purchases are bound to a one-way hash of your signed-in player ID.
  • Game progress. Saves, settings, scores, and the leaderboard entries you submit.
  • Purchases. The products you buy and the store's transaction records (Google Play order IDs and a one-way hash of the purchase token, which our server sends to Google to verify the purchase; App Store transaction and original transaction IDs), kept in our purchase ledger so a purchase is granted once and can be restored. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Rewarded ads. Records of ads you chose to watch for a reward, so the reward is granted once.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device and your notification choices, including any consent to advertising messages and when you gave it.
  • Technical data. App version, platform, coarse device type, and error details needed to operate and fix the game.
  • Support. Messages you send us.

How We Use Information

To run the game and its online features, keep your progress and purchases, grant purchases and rewards exactly once, prevent fraud and cheating, send the notifications you asked for, answer support requests, and meet store and legal requirements.
